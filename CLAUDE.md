- Video flow (default, voice=elevenlabs): same four-shot walkaround prompt, but the user first approves an ElevenLabs narration for the car (Narration panel). Its MP3 is sent as Seedance `audio_urls` (promptNative mode 'speech', speaking window = narration length) to guide voice, words and pace; a long silent end made Seedance pad by repeating lines (the price said twice), so 30-second previews are read slower (`narrationVoiceSettings`, config `narration_speed`, default 0.9), the prompt says each sentence exactly once, and when 3 s or more remain after the speech the four presenter shots end with the speech and one or two camera-only pan shots of the car (nobody on screen or speaking) fill the rest; the final video KEEPS Seedance's own speech because its lips match it (user verdict after testing: swapping in the ElevenLabs MP3 made lip sync very bad). Captions are timed from the video's own audio. The swap code remains behind a per-job `audio_swap` flag. Words Seedance mispronounces are respelled inside the spoken line (`SEEDANCE_RESPELL`, overridable with config `respell`; San Ruh-mone was still wrong in the first test, default is now San Rah-moan; next fallback San Ra-MOAN, then drop the city from the spoken line); captions keep the real spelling. Captions: `captionCues` splits at sentence ends and commas and matches audio pauses to those boundaries (`captionAnchors`, DP); the car name keeps up to three trim words after it. Seedance often adds or repeats words ("a Subaru X", the price twice), which made script-timed captions drift and linger; so when config `whisper_cli`/`whisper_model` are set, `goldenHeardWords` runs local whisper.cpp (free, ~40 s) on the video's speech and `captionSpokenScript` aligns it to the script (captions always show exactly the approved caption text; words she added are marked CAPTION_EXTRA and used only for timing, never shown) before `captionCues` times it. Whisper's own word times are too loose to use directly, so `captionRetime` (after `captionCues`, in `goldenNativeCues`) snaps each cue's first/last recognized word to the measured speech spans (a comma said without a pause used to shift every later caption by one phrase: 2013 Subaru take, 2026-10-18); a repeated word matches its earliest saying; cues under 1 s join a neighbour in the same sentence, rewrapped to up to 3 lines (`captionWrapLines`, no line ending on a CAPTION_GLUE word). Native-voice caption text is FontSize 11 (was 13; user's choice after comparing). No whisper or <60% matched → the written script is timed as before. Spoken text says years and comma numbers as words (`numberSpeech`); `speechWordCount` keeps counting them as written so the tuned 55–60 word budget is unchanged.
- Video flow (voice=native, Seedance voice): Seedance 2.5 `generate_audio` true with `promptNative()` — four wide shots around the whole parked car (front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, front from the other side); the entire car is in frame and in focus in every shot and the spokesperson walks around it beside the car, never between camera and car (user feedback after two test renders). She speaks the script word for word (nativeSpeech form: 'dot net', pronunciation guide for San Ramon). Optional config voice_reference_url is sent as Seedance audio_urls for a consistent voice.
